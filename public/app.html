Stream overlay

In OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio add a Browser (or Link) source with that address, size 1920 × 1080. Gift popups show on your stream.

This is your overlay. Drag a box to move it (gift popups too), drag its round corner to make it bigger or smaller. Your real overlay (in TikTok LIVE Studio) moves too. Full screen makes it bigger to work on.

Boxes on the overlay

Show or hide each box on your stream, and make it wider, narrower, taller or shorter (the words fit inside). You can also drag the side handles of a box in the overlay above. Auto = the box's own size. Hidden boxes stay faded in the overlay above so you can still place them.

Gift popups

How the popup looks when a gift comes in. Changes show in the overlay above straight away.

Gift list on the overlay

Each gift in the game marked LIVE, its price and what it does. It fills itself in and changes when you switch game, type or gifts. Gift popups show in the middle, so keep the list at a side (or tick "Fade out during popups").

    2. 
      
  2. Click in the address box and press Ctrl + V: the overlay's address is already copied.
    3. 
      
  3. Set the size to 1920 × 1080 (or Fit to screen) and press Add.
    4. 
      
  4. Drag it to the top of your sources so the popups show over the game.
    5.
