GIFT HYPE
=========
TikTok LIVE gifts play sounds, pop up on your stream, and make things happen in your games.

OPEN IT
  Double-click "Gift Hype" on your desktop. It opens in its own window.
  Closing the window closes Gift Hype.
  (First time on a new PC: run Setup.bat once. It installs what's needed and adds the desktop icon.)

GO LIVE
  1. Type your TikTok name at the top and press Connect.
     It keeps trying until your live starts, and connects by itself next time.
  2. Click the cover of the game you're playing (it says LIVE).
  3. Pick its type at the top, e.g. Minecraft: Parkour Paradise or Block Delete Survival.
  4. Every gift you set up for that type now plays its sound and does its actions.

GAMES
  The games screen shows a cover for every game with a TikTok LIVE mod. Right now that's Minecraft.
  Click a cover to open that game. "All games" takes you back. "Change cover" sets your own picture.
  TYPES: Minecraft has Parkour Paradise and Block Delete Survival. Each type has its own gifts.
    The type you pick is the one gifts use. "+ Type", Rename and Remove change the list.
  YOUR OWN WORLD (the easy way): Minecraft -> "Singleplayer world" -> pick your world and tick the EULA box.
    Gift Hype opens that world on a hidden server on your PC (first time it downloads Minecraft's
    official server). In Minecraft go to Multiplayer and join "Gift Hype" (or localhost:25566).
    It's your world with your stuff; gifts happen instantly. Don't open it in Singleplayer at the
    same time. Closing Gift Hype saves and closes the world.
  SERVER: Minecraft -> "Server" runs commands on your server. Pick your server from the list:
    Gift Hype finds it on your PC and connects by itself, no password to type.
    If it says gift commands are off, press the pink button and restart that server once.
  "+ New game" adds another game: Minecraft, Key presses (presses keys in whatever window
  is in front) or Sounds only (just sounds and stream popups).

MINECRAFT'S TYPES
  Click Minecraft on the Games screen and you pick a type: Dropper, Block Delete Survival or Parkour
  Paradise, each with its own cover and its own gifts. The one marked LIVE is where gifts go. On a
  type's page, "Change cover" changes that type's picture.

THE DROPPER (Minecraft -> Dropper)
  100 dropper levels in 10 chapters (Dawn Gardens, Sunstone Halls, Tidal Vaults, Cherry Grove,
  Frostpeak, Ember Depths, Verdant Wilds, Clockwork Foundry, Shadow Realm, Golden Ascent), each level
  with its own name and design, slowly getting harder. Gift Hype makes its own world for it
  ("Gift Hype Dropper") and builds the levels the first time (a few minutes). Press Play Minecraft,
  then Play in the launcher.
  Each level starts in a garden on top: walk down the catwalk and drop in. Land in the water at the
  bottom and you go straight to the next level; touch anything else and you start that level again.
  "LEVEL 07 / 100" is at the top of your screen, fails and punches ON/OFF at the bottom, and the reset
  countdown under the level bar. You start on a bridge on one side and drop off the end; you only
  go up a level when all of you lands in the water.
  On the stream overlay (see ON STREAM) each gift pops up with its picture on spinning sun rays, the
  sender's name and what it did ("punched you x2", "+5 levels"). During a reset countdown a box shows
  the seconds left and "Send Glowing Jellyfish to SAVE me!", and the reset's sound (I Need a Hero)
  plays on for the whole countdown until someone saves you. Jail locks you in a cage on your level's
  bridge.
  Starting gifts: Finger Heart = punch arrow (only while punches are on), Doughnut = punches on/off,
    Rosa = jail 5s, Paper Crane / Corgi = up 1 / 5 levels, Hat and Mustache / Boxing Gloves = down 1 / 5,
    Galaxy = 60s reset countdown (back to level 1), Glowing Jellyfish = saves you (or a shield if no
    countdown is running), Hand Hearts = level slot machine. Change any of them like other gifts.
  Level slot machine: a slot machine spins on the stream overlay (about 4 seconds, ticking as the
    numbers go past) and stops on one of the amounts you typed for that gift (e.g. -10, -5, -1, 1, 5, 10).
    Green + sends you up that many levels, red - sends you down. Numbers are in Monocraft, a free
    Minecraft-style font (publiconts, SIL Open Font License).

GIFTS
  + Add gift / Browse all gifts   - every TikTok gift with its picture and price
  + Any other gift                - plays for any gift you didn't set up
  Each gift has: a sound (and volume), "Pop up on stream", what it does, and actions.
  Actions: press "+ Add an action..." and pick one. For Minecraft there are ready-made ones:
    Nuke (falls from the sky, goes up in a mushroom cloud and leaves a crater down to y -20 with a steep wall you can climb;
      crater size 40 = a hole about 290 blocks across, 18 chunks), Jail (pick the seconds),
    Cobweb / Lava / Air chunk, Clear inventory, Teleport to the End, God kit, Diamond kit,
    Spawn a mob (any mob, pick how many), Lightning strike.
    "Custom command" is still there to type your own Minecraft command.
    A nuke removes around 200,000 blocks, so the server can freeze for a few seconds.
  "Once per gift in a streak" = a streak of 5 Roses does the actions 5 times.
  Test tries it right away without going live.

SOUNDS
  30 real meme sounds to start (fahh, bruh, vine boom, MLG airhorn, metal pipe, DaBaby "let's go", here comes the money, I need a hero...).
  They download by themselves the first time Gift Hype opens (needs internet once).
  Find more: type in "Find any sound" (e.g. "emotional damage"), press play to listen, Add to keep it.
  Add your own: drop .mp3 / .wav files on "Add your own sounds", or press "upload..." next to a gift.
  They're saved in the sounds\custom folder.

ON STREAM (OBS / TikTok LIVE Studio)
  Press "Set up in TikTok LIVE Studio" (Settings, Stream overlay): it copies the address, opens LIVE
  Studio and shows the 4 clicks to add it. Or by hand: TikTok LIVE Studio: add a Link source, OBS: add
  a Browser source, with
    http://gifthype.localtest.me:4747/overlay   size 1920 x 1080.
  (localtest.me always points back to your own PC, so it is still only on this PC. TikTok LIVE Studio
  won't take a "localhost" address. In OBS http://localhost:4747/overlay works too.)
  If your streaming app doesn't hear the Gift Hype window, set "Play sounds in" to the OBS overlay.

MAKING CHANGES
  Change anything in the app itself (games, gifts, sounds, settings): it saves by itself.
  Change how the app works: ask Claude in the Parkour TT project. The files are in this folder.
  After a change, close the Gift Hype window and open it again from the desktop.
