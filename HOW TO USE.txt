GIFT HYPE
=========
TikTok LIVE gifts play sounds, pop up on your stream, and make things happen in your games.

OPEN IT
  Double-click "Gift Hype" on your desktop. It opens in its own window.
  Closing the window closes Gift Hype.
  (First time on a new PC: run Setup.bat once. It installs what's needed and adds the desktop icon.)

GO LIVE
  1. Type your TikTok name at the top and press Connect.
     It keeps trying until your live starts, and connects by itself next time.
  2. Click the cover of the game you're playing (it says LIVE).
  3. Pick its type at the top, e.g. Minecraft: Parkour Paradise or Block Delete Survival.
  4. Every gift you set up for that type now plays its sound and does its actions.

GAMES
  The games screen shows a cover for every game with a TikTok LIVE mod. Right now that's Minecraft.
  Click a cover to open that game. "All games" takes you back. "Change cover" sets your own picture.
  TYPES: Minecraft has Parkour Paradise and Block Delete Survival. Each type has its own gifts.
    The type you pick is the one gifts use. "+ Type", Rename and Remove change the list.
  YOUR OWN WORLD (the easy way): Minecraft -> "Singleplayer world" -> pick your world and tick the EULA box.
    Gift Hype opens that world on a hidden server on your PC (first time it downloads Minecraft's
    official server). In Minecraft go to Multiplayer and join "Gift Hype" (or localhost:25566).
    It's your world with your stuff; gifts happen instantly. Don't open it in Singleplayer at the
    same time. Closing Gift Hype saves and closes the world.
  SERVER: Minecraft -> "Server" runs commands on your server. Pick your server from the list:
    Gift Hype finds it on your PC and connects by itself, no password to type.
    If it says gift commands are off, press the pink button and restart that server once.
  "+ New game" adds another game: Minecraft, Key presses (presses keys in whatever window
  is in front) or Sounds only (just sounds and stream popups).

MINECRAFT'S TYPES
  Click Minecraft on the Games screen and you pick a type: Dropper, Block Delete Survival, Chunk Chat,
  Chat Teleport or Parkour Paradise, each with its own cover and its own gifts. The one marked LIVE is
  where gifts go. On a type's page, "Change cover" changes that type's picture.

CHAT TELEPORT (Minecraft -> Chat Teleport)
  Viewers type anything in Minecraft in your LIVE chat, and every 30 seconds you're teleported to the
  nearest one:
    - a block: every block there is (chest, diamonds, grass block, spawner, beacon, oak log...), and
      groups like "logs" or "diamonds". First the land around you (96 blocks, the whole height of the
      world); if it isn't there, Gift Hype asks Minecraft for the nearest biome or structure that block
      is found in (cactus: deserts; bell: villages; spawner: mineshafts, fortresses...), loads it and
      looks there: up to 50,000 blocks away ("Look for blocks up to"). Nothing is loaded ahead of
      time: Minecraft knows where biomes and structures are without loading the land, so only the
      spot it lands on is loaded. Further away takes longer. The box says "LOOKING..." meanwhile. You land standing on it. Nothing in the
      world is changed to find it.
    - a structure: all 34 (village, trial chamber, ancient city, stronghold, woodland mansion, witch
      hut, ocean monument, shipwreck, ruined portal, mineshaft, nether fortress, bastion, end city...).
      Underground ones (trial chambers, ancient cities, strongholds, mineshafts) put you down in them.
    - a biome: all 65 (cherry grove, deep dark, ice spikes, mushroom island, crimson forest, end
      highlands...). Cave biomes put you in the cave.
  Names don't have to be exact: typos ("spawnr", "vilage", "trail chamber"), part of a name
  ("crafting", "enchanting"), words in any order ("warm ocean ruin"), split words ("strong hold") and
  ore words ("diamond", "iron" = that ore) all work. Everyday chat ("lol", "gg", "hello") and gift
  names ("rose", "hearts", "galaxy") are ignored.
  If you'd land inside blocks (buried ores, rock, lava), only the 2 blocks you take up (feet and head)
  are cleared, so you can breathe; nothing round you is touched.
  You're never sent to another dimension: only things in the dimension you're in count. In the
  Overworld nobody can pick a nether fortress or the End; in the Nether, a village or a cherry grove
  is refused. Blocks are always looked for in the dimension you're in.
  The thing typed MOST wins (one vote per viewer each round), or the LAST one typed. If it isn't near
  you, the next one is tried. A box fixed on your Minecraft screen says "TELEPORT IN" and the seconds
  left (it never says what you're going to or how many votes). After the teleport it shows what you
  went to, with a picture of the block (3D like the inventory, or flat: "3D block pictures"), and how
  many blocks away it was. "Box left / right",
  "Box down / up" and "Box size" set where it sits and how big it is (untick "Box in Minecraft" to hide
  it). If you change its size (or "3D block pictures") while you're in the world, the
  picture shows again the next time you join: Gift Hype reopens the world once you've left. The box comes in a small resource pack Gift Hype gives Minecraft when you join your
  world (if Minecraft asks, say Yes). On a server of your own it's plain text above the hotbar.
  The stream overlay can show it too ("Timer on the stream overlay"). "Things chat can't pick" keeps
  anything out (e.g. lava). Same gifts as Block Delete Survival. "Try" adds a vote without TikTok;
  "Teleport now" ends the round straight away.

CHUNK CHAT (Minecraft -> Chunk Chat)
  Viewers type blocks in your LIVE chat (stone, diamond block, oak leaves, water...). Every 30 seconds
  the whole chunk you're in turns into the block chat picked: every block from the bottom of the world
  to the top, except air (so you're never buried) and the bedrock floor. You choose whether the block
  typed MOST wins (each viewer has one vote per round; typing another block moves their vote) or the
  LAST block typed. Nothing about the timer shows on stream, so you can add your own in TikTok LIVE
  Studio; you only hear a sound when the chunk changes. To have Gift Hype show it, tick "Show the
  timer and the winning block on stream": a box on the overlay (top right), plus a green bar and a
  title in the game. The timer waits while you're not in the world.
  Some blocks can't be picked: what the gifts do (lava, air, cobwebs), blocks you'd fall through
  (chains, candles, torches, flowers, rails, ladders, signs, carpets, doors...), blocks with an inside
  (chests, furnaces, hoppers...), blocks that set themselves off (TNT, pistons, observers) and bedrock.
  You can add more under "Other blocks chat can't pick". The gifts are the same as Block Delete
  Survival (nuke, jail, cobweb / lava / air chunk, End, kits, mobs). "Try" adds a vote without TikTok;
  "Change the chunk now" ends the round straight away.

CHAT BLOCK DELETE (Minecraft -> Block Delete Survival)
  Viewers type a block's name in your LIVE chat, like stone, oak log, dirt or minecraft:sand. A
  countdown starts on the stream overlay (top right) and as a red bar at the top of your game. When it
  runs out, every block of that kind around you is gone, from the top of the world to the bottom.
  Some plural words mean a whole group: logs, leaves, planks, wool, flowers, slabs, stairs, stones,
  diamond ores... Anything in chat that isn't a block is ignored.
  It's on by itself for Block Delete Survival. Under the gift list you can set the countdown, how far it
  reaches (just your chunk up to 7x7 chunks), how long each viewer waits before picking again, how many
  countdowns can run at once, and the blocks chat can never delete (bedrock is on that list). Type a
  block in the Try box to test it without TikTok; "Stop all countdowns" cancels them.

THE DROPPER (Minecraft -> Dropper)
  100 dropper levels, every one a place: Flower Meadow, Candy Land, Picnic Party, Desert Tomb, Coral
  Reef, Sky Temple, Jungle Temple, Mushroom Grove, Bee Hive, Ice Spikes, Winter Gifts, Crystal Cave,
  Copper Works, Lava Forge, Nether Fortress, Haunted House, Night City, Neon Grid, Nightclub, Outer
  Space, Gold Mine, Rainbow Road and the Royal Palace (level 100). Bright and friendly first, darker
  and wilder later. Each is a huge shaft (73 to 109 blocks across) with a fall of 680 to 1380 blocks.
  Its walls belong to the theme (candy-cane spirals, picnic checks, speakers, city windows, stars,
  ice streaks, a rainbow spiral...), and its few big layers far apart are tiled to match, each with
  a giant centrepiece filling the shaft wall to wall round the spot you drop through: a starburst, sun, spiral, target, flower,
  donut, pizza, toadstool, snowflake, jack-o'-lantern, gear, gem, planet... Later on there are fake
  spots with the same centrepiece. The landing floor matches too (checks, a dance floor, confetti),
  with the pool in a centrepiece. The spots never line up: you steer the whole way down. A few
  levels are much harder than the ones round them. On the bridge, "FAILS HERE" counts how many times
  you've failed that level; entering a level shows its theme and name, and every 10 levels there's a
  "LEVELS DONE!" banner. The levels climb as you go: level 1 starts at y=600 and
  each level a little higher, up to y=1200 at level 100. Gift Hype makes its own world for it ("Gift Hype Dropper 4", reaching from y=-512 up to y=1535, on Minecraft 26.2) and builds the levels
  the first time. That takes up to an hour; the Dropper type shows how far it is. If it stops (you
  close Gift Hype or switch type), it carries on from the same level next time. Press Play Minecraft,
  then Play in the launcher.
  ("Gift Hype Dropper", "Gift Hype Dropper 2" and "Gift Hype Dropper 3", the older Dropper worlds,
  aren't used any more. You can delete them in Minecraft's Singleplayer list.)
  SECRET LEVEL 101: nothing shows it's there (the bar says LEVEL 100 / 100). Beat level 100 and
  instead of the winners' platform you're taken to level 101, "Ascension", at y=1500: the hardest
  level (black, purple and gold, 7 layers, an 850 block fall, a 1x1 pool). You get one try: touch
  anything and you're back on level 100. Level-up gifts and the slot machine can't take you there or
  move you on it. Land in its pool and you've beaten every level.
  Each level starts on a small bridge at the top: walk to the end and drop in. Land in the water at the
  bottom and you go straight to the next level; touch anything else and you start that level again.
  "LEVEL 07 / 100" is at the top of your screen, fails and punches ON/OFF at the bottom, and the reset
  countdown under the level bar. You start on a bridge on one side and drop off the end; you only
  go up a level when all of you lands in the water.
  On the stream overlay (see ON STREAM) each gift pops up with its picture on spinning sun rays, the
  sender's name and what it did ("punched you x2", "+5 levels"). During a reset countdown a box shows
  the seconds left and "Send Glowing Jellyfish to SAVE me!", and the reset's sound (I Need a Hero)
  plays on for the whole countdown until someone saves you. Jail locks you in a cage on your level's
  bridge.
  Starting gifts: Finger Heart = punch arrow (only while punches are on), Doughnut = punches on/off,
    Rosa = jail 5s, Paper Crane / Corgi = up 1 / 5 levels, Hat and Mustache / Boxing Gloves = down 1 / 5,
    Galaxy = 60s reset countdown (back to level 1), Glowing Jellyfish = saves you (or a shield if no
    countdown is running), Hand Hearts = level slot machine. Change any of them like other gifts.
  Level slot machine: a slot machine spins on the stream overlay (about 4 seconds, ticking as the
    numbers go past) and stops on one of the amounts you typed for that gift (e.g. -10, -5, -1, 1, 5, 10).
    Green + sends you up that many levels, red - sends you down. Numbers are in Monocraft, a free
    Minecraft-style font (publiconts, SIL Open Font License).

GIFTS
  + Add gift / Browse all gifts   - every TikTok gift with its picture and price
  + Any other gift                - plays for any gift you didn't set up
  Each gift has: a sound (and volume), "Pop up on stream", what it does, and actions.
  Actions: press "+ Add an action..." and pick one. For Minecraft there are ready-made ones:
    Nuke (falls from the sky, goes up in a mushroom cloud and leaves a crater down to y -20 with a steep wall you can climb;
      crater size 40 = a hole about 290 blocks across, 18 chunks), Jail (pick the seconds),
    Cobweb / Lava / Air chunk, Clear inventory, Teleport to the End, God kit, Diamond kit,
    Spawn a mob (any mob, pick how many), Lightning strike.
    "Custom command" is still there to type your own Minecraft command.
    A nuke removes around 200,000 blocks, so the server can freeze for a few seconds.
  "Once per gift in a streak" = a streak of 5 Roses does the actions 5 times.
  Test tries it right away without going live.

SOUNDS
  30 real meme sounds to start (fahh, bruh, vine boom, MLG airhorn, metal pipe, DaBaby "let's go", here comes the money, I need a hero...).
  They download by themselves the first time Gift Hype opens (needs internet once).
  Find more: type in "Find any sound" (e.g. "emotional damage"), press play to listen, Add to keep it.
  Add your own: drop .mp3 / .wav files on "Add your own sounds", or press "upload..." next to a gift.
  They're saved in the sounds\custom folder.

ON STREAM (OBS / TikTok LIVE Studio)
  Press "Set up in TikTok LIVE Studio" (Settings, Stream overlay): it copies the address, opens LIVE
  Studio and shows the 4 clicks to add it. Or by hand: TikTok LIVE Studio: add a Link source, OBS: add
  a Browser source, with
    http://gifthype.localtest.me:4747/overlay   size 1920 x 1080.
  (localtest.me always points back to your own PC, so it is still only on this PC. TikTok LIVE Studio
  won't take a "localhost" address. In OBS http://localhost:4747/overlay works too.)
  If your streaming app doesn't hear the Gift Hype window, set "Play sounds in" to the OBS overlay.

MAKING CHANGES
  Change anything in the app itself (games, gifts, sounds, settings): it saves by itself.
  Change how the app works: ask Claude in the Parkour TT project. The files are in this folder.
  After a change, close the Gift Hype window and open it again from the desktop.
